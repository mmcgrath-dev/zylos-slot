Yes—this confirms why it still looks like a blob. The current cloud uses several large elliptical gradients, and most of them begin at full color in the center. Even without filter: blur(), those gradients blend together into one smooth purple-and-blue mass.

Replace your current nebula rules—from :root through .nebula-front::after—with this version. Keep your existing animation keyframes below it unchanged.

   NEBULA — SHARP, STRUCTURED COSMIC RIBBON
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Uses the existing:
     .nebula-back
     .nebula-mid
     .nebula-front

   No HTML changes required.

   Design goals:
   - narrow branching filaments
   - broken cloud ridges
